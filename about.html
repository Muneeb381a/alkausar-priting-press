— How it started

Our Story

Alkausar Printing Press started in 2019 from a small shop on Airport Road, Sialkot. Qari Naeem founded it with one offset machine and one clear goal — treat every customer's job as your own, and never compromise on quality.

Today we are a full-service printing house — from visiting cards to wedding invitations, flex banners to bill books, sublimation to packaging. The machinery is state-of-the-art, but the principle stays the same: zero compromise on quality.

Our clients range from local shopkeepers and corporate companies to families planning weddings. Every order gets the same care and attention — because our work is our identity.

"Printing is not just ink on paper — it is the identity of your work. We build that identity."
